The line

The K line is one wire, half duplex. Everything a tester sends comes back to it as an echo before the unit answers, so a cable that does not hear its own echo is not on the line. The usual serial format is 8 data bits, even parity, 1 stop bit.

DS2 telegrams

Used by the gearboxes, the cluster and most body modules. A telegram is the unit's address, the total length (address, length and checksum included), the data, and an XOR of every byte before it. car 

32 04 00 36                  GS20 (address 0x32), identification (00); 0x32 ^ 0x04 ^ 0x00 = 0x36
32 2E A0 ... B6              its answer; A0 = done
StatusSeen meaning
A0Done
A2Not now. The GS20 says it for 10 s after power-up or a wrong key, and when it has no program and is already in its boot loader.
B0Refused. The GS20 says it for a write to a region not erased in this session.

KWP2000 telegrams

Used by the MS45.1 DME. A telegram is B8, the target, the source, a length byte, the data and a checksum. B8 is a fixed format byte, not 0x80 plus a length, so the length is always byte 3. The tester is F1 and the DME 12. car 

B8 12 F1 02 1A 89 ..          tester to DME: read the serial number
B8 F1 12 03 7F 31 23 ..       DME to tester: routine not finished yet

A positive answer is the request's service plus 0x40. A negative one is 7F, the service, and a reason:

ReasonMeaningWhat to do
23Routine not completeAsk again. An erase says this for about 10 s.
78Answer pendingWait. The answer follows on its own.
22Conditions not correctA real refusal. An erase refuses this way when the supply voltage is too low.
80RefusedA real refusal.

Some answers are positive in form and still a refusal. The DME's key answer 71 08 00 means the key was rejected; only 71 08 01 unlocks it. See the MS45.1 session.

Cables and adapters

  • A K+DCAN USB cable carried the DME's programming session at 115200 and, with the DME's port set higher, up to 250000 cleanly and 312500 for short exchanges. Measurements. car
  • WiFi adapters need care. One common adapter firmware only does parity between 980 and 25000 baud, and its 115200 mode has no parity. The DME's programming mode is 115200 with even parity, so its answers come back torn. Asking for programming mode at 19200 instead (10 85 02) works, and the DME keeps that rate through erase and write. 38400 and above did not. car
  • Answer time grows with a slow line. At 19200 a flash block is usually answered in about 200 ms but occasionally in 670 ms, so block timeouts need to follow the negotiated P2max of 3 s, not a guess. car
