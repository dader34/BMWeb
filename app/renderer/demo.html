Vehicles and modules

Pick the car, pick a module, and the module's own menu comes up. On a desktop the default is the classic text layout, with the F-keys along the bottom; Settings switches to a modern layout with the same keys and the same screens.

Fault memory

Read the stored faults of any module, with the module's own wording and status flags, and look at the conditions the module recorded when each fault was set.

Live values

Switch positions, sensor readings and measurement blocks, refreshed continuously while the screen is open. Bars carry the module's own scale and its good and bad bands.

Activations

Drive a component from the keyboard: injectors, the fan, the fuel pump, valves. Anything that changes the car asks first and names exactly what it is about to send.

Identity and scans

Ask the car what it is, and ask every module at once whether it has anything stored.

Reference apps

Everything past this point works without a cable and without a car: the reference material from the dealer software, in the browser.

Fault lookup

Search fault codes and descriptions across every chassis and module, then open one for its service data and, where the dealer software has one, the step-by-step diagnostic procedure.

Wiring

The factory wiring diagrams, component locations, pin assignments and repair documents for each chassis. Diagrams are vector: scroll to zoom, drag to pan, and open several as tabs.

Parts

The parts catalogue by chassis: main groups, exploded diagrams, and the part numbers behind each callout.

Tuning

Open a firmware image, add a definition file, and edit named constants and tables in engineering units, with the raw hex beside them. Saving writes a file on your computer, never the car.

Job runner

For when you know the job name: pick a definition file and a job, give it an argument, and read the raw result registers. Jobs that write are marked.

Remote sessions

Share your cabled car with someone who knows the diagnostics better, or connect to theirs. The car stays on its own machine; only jobs cross, and every write is approved at the car.

Skins

Six looks for the same screens, picked in Settings. The skin changes colours and chrome only; the layout switch is separate.
